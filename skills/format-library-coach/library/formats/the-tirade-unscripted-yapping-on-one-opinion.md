# The tirade: unscripted yapping on one opinion

## Shape

The founder holds the phone on the front camera, in a car or while walking, and talks without a script about one opinion they could defend for an hour. Few cuts, high energy, real pauses. The energy is the hook, and the opinion is the payoff.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the opinion in one blunt sentence, or a promise that the rant pays off ("I'm gonna yap for a while, but by the end...").
2. 2 to 60 s: the rant. Personal examples, asides, the frustration behind the opinion.
3. 60 to 120 s: the turn. The one idea the viewer should keep, said slower and clearer.
4. Last seconds: a short landing line, then cut. No outro.

## Make it yours

Pick the topic you rant about to your cofounder after customer calls. Hit record before you lose the energy. A founder of a B2B onboarding tool yaps about why most product tours get skipped. A founder of an AI writing assistant for marketers yaps about why their audience gets bored of its own content long before customers do. A founder of a fintech for freelancers yaps about the day they realized invoicing was the real problem. You need a strong opinion and a few real examples.

## Variations

- Walk and talk: same rant while walking, the street or office changes behind you.
- Skin in the game: the founder says what they put on the line for the opinion (see the third example).
- B2B tirade: a long rant on a work topic, such as AI and team decisions (see the second example).

## Why it works

Raw delivery reads as honest, and viewers recognize their own frustration, which makes them comment and tag a colleague. The promise of a payoff opens a loop that holds attention through the long middle (Zeigarnik 1927, unfinished tasks stay in mind).

## Watch out

- Low energy kills it. Record when you feel strongly, delete the takes where you do not.
- Never film while driving. Park first.
- Long runtime only works if the payoff arrives. Trim the middle if it drifts.

## For your AI

```
Help me prepare a 60 to 150 second "tirade" (unscripted yapping) video for [company], which makes [product] for [audience].
My opinion: [the opinion I could defend for an hour]. My real examples: [2 or 3 stories from customers or my own work].
1. Write 3 blunt opening sentences, under 12 words each, or one "by the end of this" promise.
2. Give me 5 bullet prompts to glance at before I record (no full script): examples, the frustration, the one idea to keep.
3. Write the landing line, one sentence.
4. Add shot notes: front camera, parked car or walking, one or two takes, cut only dead air.
Rules: keep it unscripted, one opinion only, true examples, no invented numbers, never record while driving.
```
