# Send this to someone

## Shape

The speaker opens by naming a specific person the viewer knows ("your partner", "your CFO") and tells them to send the video to that person. Then comes a short list built for that pair: questions to ask, tests to try, lines to use. The instruction to share is part of the content.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the promise and the target in one line ("4 questions to ask your cofounder, send this to them").
2. 2 to 20 s: item one, the easiest, to show how the list works.
3. 20 to 55 s: items two and three, each a little sharper or funnier.
4. Last seconds: the last item, the boldest, and the share instruction repeated.

## Make it yours

Find the person your buyer needs to convince or work with, and write the list for that pair. A finance automation tool posts "send this to your CFO: 4 questions about month-end close". A design collaboration platform posts "3 things to ask your developer before the handoff, send it to them". An AI note-taker posts "send this to the colleague who never writes meeting notes". The share pulls your product into a conversation between two people who both matter to you.

## Variations

- Tag version: ask viewers to tag the person in the comments instead of sending privately.
- Quiz version: "answer these with your cofounder and compare".
- Reverse: "if someone sent you this, here is what they are trying to tell you".

## Why it works

The video gives the viewer a reason and a target to share it, and sending it says something about the sender, which is social currency (Berger 2013). Private shares often travel further than likes, because each one starts a conversation.

## Watch out

- Keep the list kind. A list that mocks the target turns a share into an insult.
- Avoid "share or else" pressure and engagement bait wording that platforms penalize.
- Each item must stand alone; a weak item breaks the share impulse.

## For your AI

```
Write a "send this to someone" video for [company], which helps [audience] with [problem].
1. List 5 people [audience] works or lives with who are part of [problem] (boss, cofounder, client, partner).
2. For the best target, write: the opening line naming the target and the share instruction, 4 list items (easiest first, boldest last) with one shot note each, and the closing line.
3. Give one on-screen title under 8 words.
Rules: the list stays kind to the target, no "share or else" wording, each item must stand alone.
```
