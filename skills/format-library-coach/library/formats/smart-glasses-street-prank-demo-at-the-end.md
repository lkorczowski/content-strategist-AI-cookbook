# Smart-glasses street prank (demo at the end)

## Shape

Filmed through smart glasses, the creator challenges strangers to a game or a task and wins thanks to what the glasses show or whisper. The stranger's reaction carries the video. The last seconds reveal the device and show how it worked.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: first-person shot, the stranger already in frame, and one line of on-screen text that sets the stakes ("I challenged a chess player with smart glasses on").
2. 2 to 40 s: the game starts. Cut to what the glasses see and suggest, so the viewer is in on the trick before the stranger.
3. 40 to 120 s: the stranger notices something is off. Doubt, laughter, a rematch. Keep the best reaction, cut the rest.
4. Last 10 to 20 s: the reveal. The creator shows the glasses, replays one key moment from the glasses' point of view, and says in one sentence what the tech does.

## Make it yours

An AI founder whose product sees, listens or answers in real time can run the challenge with the product inside the glasses or the phone: a translation app wins a bet in a foreign market, a sales copilot coaches a live negotiation with a street vendor, a math tutor app solves a chalkboard puzzle a passer-by sets. The reveal at the end is the product demo, so the whole video doubles as a proof of what the product does.

## Variations

- Device versus pro: the glasses against professional gear on a real set (see the second example).
- Friendly challenge: the stranger is told at the start and plays along, which removes the consent problem and keeps the demo.
- Series: one challenge per city or per profession, same opening line, so the format becomes recognizable.

## Why it works

The first-person view puts the viewer inside the trick, and the open question "will they notice?" keeps people watching until the reveal. The demo arrives when attention is highest, after the payoff, which makes it feel like part of the story.

## Watch out

- Get consent from anyone recognizable before posting, and follow local recording laws. Many platforms remove content that humiliates private people.
- Never cheat in a real competition or in anything with money at stake for the other person.
- Disclose the product if the account belongs to the company that makes it.
