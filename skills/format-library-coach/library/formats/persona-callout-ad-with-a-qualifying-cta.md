# Persona callout ad with a qualifying CTA

## Shape

The ad opens by naming one buyer role and one problem they own ("Ops leaders: your onboarding is costing you hires. Let me show you."). The founder introduces themselves, then the video shows the product or the work, the options, and four before-and-after results. The call to action filters leads: it tells viewers what to have ready before they book.

## Beats

1. 0 to 3 s: the callout of the role and the problem, three hook variants to test.
2. 3 to 15 s: the founder's introduction, in the place where the work happens.
3. 15 to 50 s: the product or service in action, the main options, then four before-and-after pairs.
4. Last 10 to 20 s: the qualifying call to action ("Come prepared with: your sites, your guidelines, the decision maker"), with a longer version for instant forms.

## Make it yours

You call out the role that signs, and you ask for the information your sales team needs. An HR software company calls out "Heads of people at 200-person companies" and asks them to bring their current tool and headcount. A cybersecurity startup calls out IT managers and asks for the number of endpoints. An AI document platform calls out legal ops leads and asks for their monthly contract volume. The qualifying ask lowers the number of leads and raises their quality, which is the point. This card was written from a real program; it stays a draft until the script itself is measured.

## Variations

- Three personas: the same body with a different callout for each buyer role.
- Organic first: post the video organically, then run the version that performed as an ad.
- Native ad: when the organic post used as an ad gets a weak click rate, switch to a version with a headline and description written for ads.

## Why it works

Hearing your own job title in the first second is a strong reason to stop scrolling. Asking viewers to come prepared signals a serious offer, so the people who book see themselves as the right fit.

## Watch out

- Before-and-after examples need the client's consent, or use your own projects.
- Keep one call to action; the qualifying list stays short (three items).
- Follow the ad platform's rules for lead forms and data collection.
