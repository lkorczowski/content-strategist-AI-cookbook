# Two founders, two paths

## Shape

Two founders who made opposite choices (bootstrap or raise, solo or cofounders) sit side by side. A host asks them the same questions and they answer in turn. Nobody declares a winner: the viewer judges and says so in the comments.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the opposition on screen ("bootstrap vs raise, which one wins?") with both founders in frame.
2. 2 to 30 s: the first question, both answers, short.
3. 30 to 75 s: two more questions, the sharpest one last, where their answers clash the most.
4. Last 10 s: a final question to the viewer: which path would you choose?

## Make it yours

You sit with another founder who made the opposite choice on a question your audience debates. You can host it yourself or be one of the two. Examples: an AI startup founder who raised a seed round facing a bootstrapped SaaS founder, a founder who sells to enterprises facing one who sells self-serve, a solo founder facing a team of three cofounders.

## Variations

- Split screen: each founder films alone, the answers cut together.
- Customer versus: two customers who chose opposite tools answer the same questions.
- Series: one opposition per episode, same set and same questions.

## Why it works

Two credible answers to the same question let viewers pick a side, and picking a side makes people comment. The practical answers give viewers lessons they can use for their own choice.

## Watch out

- Pick two founders with real experience of their path. A weak side makes the debate unfair.
- Agree with both on topics to avoid, such as investors' names or numbers they cannot share.
- Give both founders equal time in the edit.
