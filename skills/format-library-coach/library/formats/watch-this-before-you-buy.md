# Watch this before you buy

## Shape

The video lists the mistakes buyers make when they choose a product in one category. Each mistake is shown with a real install, a test or a side by side. The video ends on a short checklist the viewer can screenshot before buying.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: "Watch this before you buy [category]", with a shot of a bad result.
2. 2 to 60 s: mistake one and two, each with the bad version on screen, then the better choice.
3. 60 to 150 s: the last mistake, the one most people never think about, shown in a real test.
4. Last 15 s: the checklist on screen, three to five lines.

## Make it yours

Your buyers compare tools before they sign. You become the guide who helps them choose well, even if some of them pick someone else. You show real setups and real tests, and your own product appears as one of the options that passes the checklist. Examples: an HR software founder on the mistakes companies make when choosing a payroll tool, an AI chatbot founder on what to test before buying a support bot, a security startup on what to check before buying a password manager for a team.

## Variations

- Long form on YouTube, then three short cuts on TikTok, one per mistake.
- Install version: the same category shown through a full setup tutorial (see the second example).
- Checklist only: a 30-second version that only gives the list.

## Why it works

People about to spend money look for help, so the video meets them at the moment of decision with practical value (Berger 2013). The checklist gives them a reason to save and share it with whoever buys with them.

## Watch out

- Keep the advice fair. If every mistake points only to your product, viewers notice and stop trusting the list.
- Disclose your role when your product appears in the options.
- Use your own footage of tests and installs.

## Examples

- [Fix This Build That, YouTube (numbers not re-checked)](https://youtu.be/LdpvCepML-E): "Watch this before buying LEDs"; look at how the title speaks to people about to buy.
- [Burke BunchTV, YouTube (numbers not re-checked)](https://youtu.be/t3GG6NGCCJY): an install tutorial for the same category, the install variation.

## For your AI

```
Plan a "watch this before you buy" video for [company], which sells [product] in the [category] market to [audience].
1. List the 5 most common mistakes [audience] makes when choosing a [category] tool, with the consequence of each.
2. Keep the 3 strongest. For each, describe the test or real setup that shows the bad version and the better choice.
3. Write the opening line, the script with shot notes and the final checklist (3 to 5 lines).
4. Give a long version (up to 180 s) and 3 short cuts of 30 to 45 s.
Rules: fair advice that holds for any vendor, disclose that I run [company], no invented numbers, only footage we film.
```
