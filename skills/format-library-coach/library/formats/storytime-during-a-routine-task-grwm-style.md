# Storytime during a routine task (GRWM style)

## Shape

The speaker does a mundane task on camera (getting ready, making coffee, packing orders) while telling a personal story with a cliffhanger. The task gives the eyes something to follow, and the story gives the ears a reason to stay. The task and the story end together.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the hook of the story and the task already started ("Pack orders with me while I tell you how we lost our biggest customer").
2. First quarter: the setup of the story, while the task begins.
3. Middle: the turn. The worst moment, told slowly, as the task continues.
4. Ending: the resolution and the task finished at the same time, with a last line on what you learned.

## Make it yours

Pick a task you do anyway and a story that has a real twist. A founder of an AI inbox assistant makes coffee while telling the story of the email that almost cost them a funding round. A founder of an e-commerce analytics tool packs orders for a side shop while telling how they found a fraud pattern. A founder of a remote hiring platform gets ready for a call while telling about the worst interview of their life. The task can show your work, your product or your desk.

## Variations

- Short cut: a 60-second version with only the hook and the twist, linking to the long one.
- Multi-part: the story stops on a cliffhanger and continues in part 2.
- Work task: the routine task is the product in use (setting up a dashboard while telling the story).

## Why it works

The hands-on task keeps the eyes busy while the story builds, which helps viewers stay through long runtimes. A cliffhanger opens a loop the viewer wants to close (Zeigarnik 1927), and a personal story told with feeling matches the stories driver of Berger 2013.

## Watch out

- The story needs a twist. Without one, the task alone will lose people.
- Get consent before telling a story that involves a customer, an investor or an ex-employee who could be identified.
- Long videos need clear chapters or captions so viewers can follow.

## For your AI

```
Plan a "storytime during a routine task" video for [company], which makes [product] for [audience]. Target length: [1 to 20 minutes].
The task: [what I will do on camera]. The story: [what happened, the worst moment, how it ended].
1. Write 3 opening lines that name both the task and the story hook, under 20 words each.
2. Split the story into 4 beats (hook, setup, turn, resolution) and match each beat to a stage of the task.
3. Mark where to put a cliffhanger if I split it into parts.
4. Add shot notes: fixed facecam, task in frame, captions for chapters.
Rules: true story, anonymize or get consent for anyone identifiable, the product appears only if it fits the task, no invented numbers.
```
