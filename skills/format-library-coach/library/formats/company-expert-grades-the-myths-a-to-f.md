# Company expert grades the myths (A to F)

## Shape

The company's expert (chief scientist, CTO, head of research) reads popular claims of the category one by one. Each claim gets a letter grade and one sentence of evidence. The video is posted from the brand account, often as a recurring series.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the expert introduced by role and the promise ("Our CTO is back to grade more AI myths").
2. 2 to 15 s: first claim on screen, the grade, one sentence of evidence.
3. 15 to 60 s: three or four more claims, faster, with at least one surprising grade (a popular claim that fails, or a dismissed one that passes).
4. Last seconds: the best grade of the day, and a call for claims to grade next time.

## Make it yours

Put the person in your team with the deepest expertise on camera, and let them speak as the expert they are. A sleep-tracking app has its research lead grade sleep hacks. A cybersecurity startup has its CTO grade password advice. An AI nutrition coach has its dietitian grade diet trends. The brand account gets a recurring face, and the expert's grades build trust in what the product recommends.

## Variations

- Scale change: grades from 0 to 10 instead of letters, or green, yellow, red.
- Viewer-sourced: each episode grades claims taken from the comments.
- Founder as expert: the founder grades the claims when they hold the expertise.

## Why it works

Each new claim opens a small question (what grade will it get?), and the quick answer rewards the viewer before the next one starts. A surprising grade gives people something to correct their friends with, which Berger 2013 calls practical value.

## Watch out

- Each grade needs real evidence. The expert's credibility is the whole format.
- Health, finance and legal claims carry platform rules on misinformation: cite sources.
- The account belongs to the brand, so keep product claims accurate and disclosed.

```
Write a 45 to 75 second "company expert grades the myths" script for [company], which makes [product] for [audience].
Our expert: [name, role, field of expertise]. The category: [category].
1. List 8 popular claims in [category] that people repeat online.
2. For the 5 best, give a letter grade (A to F) and one sentence of evidence each, with the source I should check.
3. Order them so one surprising grade comes in the middle.
4. Write the opening line (expert role plus promise) and the closing call for claims to grade next.
5. Add shot notes: facecam, claim and grade as on-screen text, cut on each claim.
Rules: real evidence for each grade, flag any health, finance or legal claim that needs a citation, no invented numbers.
```
