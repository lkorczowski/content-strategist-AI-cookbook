# Customer testimonial in UGC style

## Shape

A real customer films themselves with a phone, at home or at work. In their own words they tell how things were before, the moment it changed and what is different now. The framing stays simple, with maybe one shot of the product in use.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the customer's strongest sentence about the result or the old pain, straight to camera.
2. 2 to 20 s: the before. What was hard, in their words, with one concrete detail.
3. 20 to 45 s: the turn. When they started using the product and the first moment they noticed a difference, with a screen or product shot if possible.
4. Last 10 s: where they are now and who they would recommend it to.

## Make it yours

You ask your happiest customers to film a short video on their phone, and you send them three questions instead of a script: what was hard before, when did it change, what is different now. Then you edit lightly. Examples: a clinic manager talking about an AI scheduling tool that ended the phone chaos at the front desk, a solo founder showing the dashboard of an AI bookkeeping tool at tax time, a recruiter explaining how an interview notes assistant changed their week.

## Variations

- Screen version: the customer records their screen while talking over it.
- Short cut: one sentence of before, one of after, 15 seconds, for ads.
- Series: one customer per week, same three questions, same opening card.

## Why it works

A real person describing a real problem triggers recognition in people who have the same problem. The before and after structure is a small story, and stories carry trust better than claims (Berger 2013).

## Watch out

- Only real customers, telling their real experience. Never script results they did not get.
- Paid or rewarded testimonials must be disclosed, including free months or gifts.
- Get written consent to use the video, and agree on where it will run (organic, ads, site).
