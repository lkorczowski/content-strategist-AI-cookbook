# Creator skit with the product woven in

## Shape

A creator runs their usual comedy skit, with the same characters and tone their audience knows. The product appears as a natural part of the story: it solves the problem in the plot, causes the twist or becomes the running gag. The partnership is disclosed, and the skit ends on a joke that brings the product back once more.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the skit opens like any other video from the creator, a recognizable character or situation.
2. 2 to 40 s: the story builds. The product enters as a prop the characters need.
3. 40 to 90 s: the twist. The product changes the outcome of the scene, and its key feature is shown in action.
4. Last 10 s: the punchline, with a final callback to the product.

## Make it yours

You hire a creator whose humour already fits your buyers, then you hand them the problem your product solves and let them write the scene. Your brief gives one feature and one moment of use. Their skit does the rest. Examples: an AI meeting assistant that saves a character who forgot everything from the last call, a password manager that becomes the running gag in an office sitcom, a language app that turns a misunderstanding abroad into the punchline. The [performance-creator-program](../../../performance-creator-program/) skill covers how to recruit and brief creators for this.

## Variations

- Series: the product returns in several episodes of the creator's recurring story (see the phone example).
- Tutorial skit: the skit pretends to teach something absurd and the product is the tool (see the shoe example).
- Founder cameo: you appear as a side character for one line.

## Why it works

The audience watches for the creator and the story, so the product is seen by people who skip classic ads. A product that drives the plot is remembered because it is tied to the joke.

## Watch out

- Disclose the partnership with the platform's label and in the caption, every time.
- Let the creator keep their voice. A script written by your brand team breaks the trust the audience has in them.
- Agree in writing on usage rights before you reuse the skit as an ad.

## For your AI

```
Write a creator brief and 3 skit ideas for [company], which sells [product] to [audience].
1. Describe the creator profile whose humour fits [audience] (tone, recurring characters, platform).
2. Give the one feature of [product] the skit must show and the problem it solves in real life.
3. Propose 3 skit concepts where [product] drives the plot: opening situation, how the product enters, the twist, the punchline.
4. For the best concept, write a beat sheet (0 to 2 s, body, twist, ending) with shot notes for a multi-role edit, 50 to 110 seconds.
Rules: the creator keeps their voice, the partnership is disclosed with the platform label, no invented claims, usage rights agreed before any paid reuse.
```
