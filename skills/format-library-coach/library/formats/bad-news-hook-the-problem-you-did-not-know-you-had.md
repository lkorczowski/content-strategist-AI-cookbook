# Bad news hook: the problem you did not know you had

## Shape

The video opens with "I got bad news" and reveals a hidden flaw in something the viewer uses every day. It proves the flaw visually, with a close-up, a slow-motion or an animated graph. Then it introduces the product that fixes the flaw and shows the difference.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: "I got bad news" and the everyday object or tool on screen.
2. 2 to 30 s: the hidden flaw, made visible: raw data, a slow-motion, an animation that shows what the eye misses.
3. 30 to 70 s: why the flaw happens, explained simply, then the product that fixes it, shown with the same visual proof.
4. Last 5 to 15 s: the side by side, before and after the fix, and the product name.

## Make it yours

The flaw must live in the tool your buyer uses today, and your product must remove it measurably. A password manager shows how often a browser autofills a password on a look-alike domain. An email deliverability tool shows how many newsletters land in spam, using a real test inbox. A backup startup shows what a common cloud sync does when a file is deleted on one device. Motion design or a clean screen recording makes the invisible visible. This format costs time to produce, so keep it for your strongest technical proof.

## Variations

- Founder version: the founder delivers the bad news to camera, then cuts to the visual proof.
- Viewer test: give viewers a way to check the flaw on their own setup before you show the fix.
- Series: one flaw per video in the same category.

## Why it works

Bad news about something you own triggers concern and the need to know whether it applies to you, which holds attention (Loewenstein 1994). A flaw proven with a visual is useful to warn others about, so people share it (Berger 2013).

## Watch out

- The flaw must be real, measurable and shown with your method. Fear without proof gets debunked in the comments.
- Name competitors with care: comparative claims carry legal risk. Describe the category instead.
- Disclose that the account belongs to the company.
