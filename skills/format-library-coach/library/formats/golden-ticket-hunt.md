# Golden ticket hunt

## Shape

The creator announces in the first seconds that a prize is hidden somewhere the audience must look: in the comments, inside a product order, or in a frame of the video. They show the prize and state the rule. The rest of the video teases where it might be and pushes people to search.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the hook and the rule together ("the first golden ticket is hidden in the comments").
2. 2 to 30 s: show the prize and how to claim it.
3. 30 to 70 s: hints, the hiding place glimpsed, the countdown or the limit.
4. Last seconds: where to find the official rules, and when the winner is announced.

## Make it yours

A SaaS or AI product can hide its ticket in places your users already go. A design tool hides a free annual plan code inside a template in its public gallery. An AI writing assistant hides a ticket in one of the comments under its launch video. A developer tool hides a code in its changelog or docs and films the team hiding it. The hunt sends people into your product or your content, which is where you want them.

## Variations

- In the product: the ticket is inside an order or a signup flow (see the second example).
- In the video: a code flashes in one frame and viewers rewatch to find it.
- Series: one ticket per day for a week, each with a new hiding place.

## Why it works

The hunt turns watching into a game with a reward, and the hidden location is a question the viewer wants answered (Loewenstein 1994). Finding the ticket or telling friends about the hunt gives social currency (Berger 2013).

## Watch out

- Contest laws apply: write official rules, state eligibility and dates, and offer a free entry route where the law requires it.
- Follow each platform's giveaway rules, and disclose the brand behind the giveaway.
- Plan moderation: a hunt in the comments brings spam and fake winner accounts.

## For your AI

```
Plan a "golden ticket hunt" video for [company], whose product is [product] for [audience].
1. Propose 5 hiding places linked to our product or content (comments, templates, docs, signup flow, a frame of the video).
2. For the best one, write: the opening line that states the rule, how to show the prize, 3 hints, and the closing line pointing to the official rules.
3. List what the official rules must state (eligibility, dates, how the winner is chosen, free entry route if required).
Rules: comply with contest law and platform giveaway rules, disclose the brand, plan comment moderation, no invented prize values.
```
