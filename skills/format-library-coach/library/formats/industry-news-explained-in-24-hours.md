# Industry news explained in 24 hours

## Shape

One piece of news from the field, posted within a day of it breaking. The speaker stands in front of the headline or the source document and says what happened, why it happened, and what it changes for the viewer. The video ends on the one thing to watch next.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the news in one line, with the headline or document behind you.
2. 2 to 30 s: what happened, the facts only.
3. 30 to 90 s: why it happened, and what it changes for your audience, with one detail from the source.
4. Last seconds: what to watch next, or a question for the comments.

## Make it yours

Speed is the whole game, so you need a short list of sources you check every morning. An AI compliance startup explains a new regulation the day it passes. A fintech founder explains what a large company's public filing reveals. A tool for app developers explains why a platform removed a popular app. Your advantage is context: you know why this matters to your buyers when general news accounts only report it.

## Variations

- Document deep dive: read the actual filing, policy or report and point to the key lines (see the third example).
- Daily roundup: several short news items in one video, same format every day (see the second example).
- Drama in your niche: the story of a small company or maker caught in a platform decision (see the first example).

## Why it works

Sharing news early makes the sharer look informed and ahead of their peers (Berger 2013, social currency). Viewers who work in the field follow an account that saves them reading time every day.

## Watch out

- Check facts against the primary source before posting. Speed never excuses a wrong fact.
- Separate facts from your opinion out loud ("here is what happened, here is what I think").
- Use headlines and screenshots within fair use and platform rules, and credit the outlet.
