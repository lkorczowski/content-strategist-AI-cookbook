# Comment reply with a full video

## Shape

One comment appears on screen through the platform's reply-with-video sticker. The speaker reads it, then answers it fully in one video. When a thread has hundreds of comments, the speaker picks the most recurring question and says so in the first line.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the comment sticker on screen and the first sentence that frames it ("Someone in my comments told me...").
2. 2 to 20 s: why this question matters, or why so many people asked it.
3. 20 to 70 s: the answer, with one example, one screen or one number from your own data.
4. Last seconds: the next question you will answer, or a call to ask more in the comments.

## Make it yours

Your comments are a free list of what your buyers want to know. You can reply as the founder or as the team: an AI bookkeeping tool for freelancers answers "does it work with my bank?", a no-code app builder answers "can I export my code?", a hiring platform for restaurants answers "why do candidates ghost?". Pick the comment that ten other people are thinking. For a full method on sorting and answering comments, see [comment-section-coach](../../../comment-section-coach/).

## Variations

- Comment reply with the data on screen: the answer is shot as greenscreen over a chart, a page or a screen (see the fourth example).
- FAQ of the season: an expert answers the question everyone asks at this time of year (see the fifth example).
- Community question to the founder: a question about the company itself, such as a partnership people want.

## Why it works

The comment on screen is social proof that real people asked, and it opens a question the viewer wants closed (Loewenstein 1994). Every reply invites more comments, which feeds the next video.

## Watch out

- Hide the commenter's name and photo if the comment is critical or personal.
- Answer the question asked. Turning every reply into a pitch kills the series.
- Keep the answer to one idea; a second question deserves a second video.

## For your AI

```
I run [company], which sells [product] to [audience]. Here are comments from my recent videos: [paste comments].
1. Group them by question and rank the groups by how often they come back.
2. For the top question, write a 30 to 90 second reply video: the opening line that frames the comment, the answer in 3 beats, and one proof from our own product or data: [what we can show].
3. Add shot notes: comment sticker placement, facecam or greenscreen, what goes behind me.
4. End with the next question I should answer.
Rules: answer the question asked, one idea per video, no pitch before the answer, hide personal details of the commenter.
```
