# New gadget explained in 60 seconds

## Shape

A new product or device is on screen in the first second. The speaker says what it does, how it works, its price or availability, and one reason it matters. The video ends on what this release means for the field.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the device in action and one line that states what it does.
2. 2 to 25 s: how it works, in plain words, over the maker's footage.
3. 25 to 50 s: price, availability, limits.
4. Last seconds: why it matters, or what it changes for the viewer.

## Make it yours

You explain releases in your own field, before you ever talk about your product. Credibility comes first. A founder of an AI tool for warehouses explains new robots. A health tech founder explains a new wearable. A developer tools founder explains a new AI coding model or chip. Pick releases your buyers will hear about this week, and add the one detail only an insider would notice. Over time, your account becomes where your market checks what is new.

## Variations

- Hands-on version: you test the device yourself instead of using the maker's footage.
- Skeptic version: same shape, ending on the one reason it might fail.
- Weekly roundup: three releases in one video, 20 seconds each.

## Why it works

A surprising device in the first frame resets attention, and the viewer wants to know how it works (Loewenstein 1994). Being first to share something new makes the sharer look early and informed (Berger 2013, social currency).

## Watch out

- Use the maker's footage within the platform's rules, credit the source on screen, and prefer your own footage when you can.
- Check every spec and price from the official source. A wrong price is the first comment you will get.
- If the maker sent you the device or paid you, disclose it.
