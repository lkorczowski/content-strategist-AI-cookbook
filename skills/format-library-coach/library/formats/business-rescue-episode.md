# Business rescue episode

## Shape

An expert arrives in a struggling business, in the style of TV rescue shows. They observe, diagnose the problems with the owner, change a few things and leave. Later they come back to measure what changed, and the owner says how it went.

## Beats

1. 0 to 30 s: the stakes. The business, the owner and the problem, with a strong quote from the owner.
2. 30 s to 5 min: the diagnosis. The expert watches the business run and names the problems one by one.
3. 5 to 15 min: the changes. Small fixes, the owner's resistance, the first test.
4. Last minutes: the return visit, what changed, the owner's words.

## Make it yours

You go into a business that has the problem your product solves and you fix it on camera, with your method and possibly your tool. The long episode lives on YouTube, and you cut the diagnosis, the conflict and the return into shorts. Examples: a restaurant software founder rescuing a struggling bistro's ordering chaos, an AI marketing tool founder fixing a local gym's empty pipeline, an inventory SaaS founder helping a small shop that runs out of stock every week.

## Variations

- Short only: diagnosis and one fix in 90 seconds.
- Remote rescue: the episode runs on video calls and screen recordings.
- Viewer nominated: followers send the business in the comments.

## Why it works

The rescue follows a full story arc with a person who has a lot at stake, which creates emotional investment. The return visit closes the loop the episode opened, so viewers stay to the end.

## Watch out

- Get the owner's and staff's written consent, and agree on what can be shown.
- Show the results the owner reports on the return visit, without promising more.
- Use your own footage. Clips from TV shows are protected and get taken down.
