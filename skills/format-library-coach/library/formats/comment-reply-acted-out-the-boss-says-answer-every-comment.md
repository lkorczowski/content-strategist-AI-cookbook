# Comment reply acted out (the boss says answer every comment)

## Shape

The video opens on the frame line "my boss says I have to respond to all the comments". A real comment appears on screen, and the team acts it out literally as a short scene in the shop or the office. Each episode takes one comment, so the frame line repeats and the series becomes recognizable.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the frame line on screen and the comment sticker, the team already in place.
2. 2 to 30 s: the setup of the scene. The comment is read, someone takes it literally.
3. 30 to 70 s: the escalation. The scene goes further than anyone expected, with the product or the workplace as the set.
4. Last seconds: a punchline, then a reaction from "the boss" or a call for the next comment.

## Make it yours

Your team and your office are the set. You pick comments that can be played as a scene, the stranger the better: an AI customer support startup acts out "can your bot handle my grandma?", a design tool team acts out "make the logo bigger", a scheduling app team acts out "what happens if two people book the same slot?". The founder can play the boss who gives the order, which keeps the founder on camera without carrying the joke.

## Variations

- One actor, several roles: a solo founder plays the employee, the customer and the boss.
- Product as a character: the app or the AI agent gets a voice and answers the comment in the scene.
- Weekly episode: same frame line, same opening shot, a new comment each week.

## Why it works

The frame line sets a game the viewer understands in one second, and every episode teaches the audience that their comment might become the next scene. That loop rewards commenting, which brings in new viewers.

## Watch out

- Get written consent from every team member who appears, and let them opt out of the series.
- Hide the commenter's name unless they agree to be shown.
- A scene that drags past the punchline loses viewers. Cut hard after the laugh.

```
Write an episode of a "my boss says I have to respond to all the comments" series for [company], a [type of business] with [team size and roles].
Comments to choose from: [paste comments].
1. Pick the 3 comments that can be acted out literally in [office, shop or set].
2. For the best one, write a 45 to 90 second scene: the opening frame line, who plays what, 3 to 5 lines of dialogue, the escalation and the punchline.
3. Add shot notes: camera position, props from our product or workplace, where the comment sticker sits.
4. Suggest the next 2 episodes.
Rules: consent from everyone on camera, no mocking the commenter, cut right after the punchline, the product appears as part of the scene.
```
