# Staged in-store discovery

## Shape

Two people walk past a place where the product is available and stop as if they just spotted it. One of them reacts with surprise ("wait, they have it here?"), picks it up and the pair tell each other why they love it. The video ends on the product in hand and where to find it.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the double take. One person stops mid-sentence and points, with a line of surprise as the first spoken words.
2. 2 to 15 s: they grab the product and show it to the camera, packaging or screen clearly visible.
3. 15 to 30 s: two or three quick reasons they love it, said like friends, each one short.
4. Last 5 s: one of them says where to get it, product held up, end on the shelf or the screen.

## Make it yours

A SaaS product has no shelf, so you stage the discovery where your buyers already are. The "shelf" can be a marketplace listing, an app store page, an integration directory or a stand at a trade show. You can shoot two coworkers at a desk who spot your tool inside software they already use: an AI note taker found in a video call app's marketplace, a scheduling tool spotted in a CRM's integration list, a hardware add-on for an AI assistant found on a store shelf. Keep the acting light and the reasons concrete.

## Variations

- Solo version: one person films with the front camera and reacts alone.
- Digital shelf: the discovery happens on a phone screen while scrolling an app store.
- Event shelf: the product is spotted on a booth at a conference.

## Why it works

The surprise in the first second resets attention and borrows the energy of a real moment between friends. It also signals availability, which is the last question a buyer asks before acting.

## Watch out

- This is an ad. Disclose it as branded content with the platform's paid partnership label and follow consumer law on hidden advertising.
- Get the store's permission before filming inside, and keep other shoppers out of frame or ask their consent.
- Overacting kills it. If the surprise feels fake, cut to the reasons faster.
