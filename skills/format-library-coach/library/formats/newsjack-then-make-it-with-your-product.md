# Newsjack, then make it with your product

## Shape

A hot news item or a meme of the day plays first, with a short reaction from the founder or a teammate. Then the team makes something about that news with the product: an object, an image, a page, a result. The video ends on the thing they made, held up or shown on screen.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the news clip, with a textbox that names the event.
2. 2 to 12 s: about 5 seconds of the clip, then about 5 seconds of reaction. Use a split screen if the clip is small.
3. 12 to 30 s: the making, fast, with the product doing the work.
4. Last seconds: the finished piece, and one line that ties it back to the news.

## Make it yours

Speed is the format. You post the same day, while the topic is still in people's feeds. An AI image tool generates a poster of the match result an hour after the final whistle. A no-code app builder ships a tiny tool about the day's viral story, such as a calculator for the new tax rule. A 3D printing service prints a miniature of the object everyone is talking about. This shape was shot in a real program, where it worked only on truly hot news and faded on evergreen topics.

## Variations

- Burst: make 3 or 4 versions the same day, keep them 24 hours, then republish only the best one on the other platforms.
- Comment version: viewers suggest the next news item to turn into a product.
- Silent version: no reaction, only the clip and the making.

## Why it works

People already care about the news, so the first seconds borrow attention that exists. Sharing a clever take on the topic of the day makes the sharer look in the loop, which is social currency (Berger 2013).

## Watch out

- Stay neutral and light on sensitive topics: tragedies, politics and religion are off limits for a product twist.
- Use only a few seconds of news footage and credit the source; TV extracts can trigger copyright strikes.
- Do not use logos or trademarks of others on the thing you make without the right to do so.

1. Propose 3 things we could make about this news with [product] in under 2 hours. Keep them funny and neutral.
