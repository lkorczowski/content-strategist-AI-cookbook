# Everyday clip with a comment trap

## Shape

An ordinary video of daily life that leaves one obvious, funny gap: something odd in the frame, a missing detail, a choice that makes no sense. Viewers rush to the comments to point it out. The top comment that fills the gap becomes the payoff, and the creator often plants it or pins it quickly.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: a normal scene with a clear caption, so the viewer knows what they are watching.
2. 2 to 30 s: the routine unfolds, and the gap is visible without anyone mentioning it.
3. 30 to 60 s: the gap gets bigger or more obvious, still unremarked on screen.
4. Last seconds: the video ends flat, and the pinned comment delivers the punchline.

## Make it yours

Film a normal moment from your work and leave one detail that your audience will love to correct. A remote team chat tool posts a "my productive morning" clip with forty unread notifications in plain view. A note-taking app founder films a calm desk tour while a wall of sticky notes covers the window behind. An AI scheduling assistant posts a weekly planning routine with three meetings booked at the same hour. Then prepare the reply videos the comments will ask for. The [comment-section-coach](../../../comment-section-coach/) skill helps you plan, pin and answer those comments.

## Variations

- Answer video: reply to the top comment with a second clip that explains the gap.
- Spot the detail: add a caption challenge ("one thing is wrong here"), which makes the trap explicit.
- Series: the same routine each week with a new gap.

## Why it works

An unexplained gap is an itch the viewer scratches by commenting, and each comment pushes the video to more people. Being the one who spotted it gives the commenter a small status win, a form of social currency (Berger 2013).

## Watch out

- If you plant or pin the first comment, write it from your own account and say who you are. Never use fake accounts.
- The gap must be harmless. A gap that looks dangerous or careless brings the wrong kind of comments.
- Keep anyone else in frame informed and consenting.
