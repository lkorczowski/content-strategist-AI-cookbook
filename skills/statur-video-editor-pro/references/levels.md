# The production dial: what each level adds and why

Research summary of 2026-10-28 (180 sources read; the public essay and handbook cite them in full). Evidence levels: measured (a study with a stated sample, so far always commissioned by a platform), platform guidance, practitioner (one creator or editor), vendor, convention, none found.

## Two kinds of production value

- Capture polish: studio set, cinematic camera, celebrity talent, outdoor shoots, perfect looks. In TikTok and Reels feeds it does little or harms: in a MAGNA x TikTok randomized test (4,477 users) high production quality was among the weakest drivers of perceived authenticity, and inauthentic native ads lost 19% of purchase intent ([MAGNA x TikTok, 2022](https://magnaglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/MAGNA-TikTok-Understanding-Strengths-of-TikTok-Ads-003.pdf)). TikTok's own guidance asks for a style that is not overly polished ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices)). An informally filmed course drew two to three times the engagement of a studio course across 6.9 million edX sessions ([Guo, Kim and Rubin, 2014](https://www.cs.rochester.edu/hci/pubs/pdfs/edX-MOOC-video-production-and-engagement_LAS-2014.pdf)).
- Edit-layer craft: aligned hooks, captions, speech with music, native graphics, proof shown with context. Measured as positive: in a Meta x Toluna study of 100 Reels ads, speech plus music made an ad 2.0x likelier to reach the top fifth for brand interest, native graphics or emojis 2.5x for purchase intent, a product shown with context 5.3x, an audio plus visual hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds 1.5x ([PPC Land summary](https://ppc.land/meta-and-toluna-study-finds-emojis-lift-reels-purchase-intent-2-5x/)).

So the dial adds attention control first (levels 2 and 3) and polish last (level 4). Limits: almost every measured study covers paid B2C ads and was paid for by a platform. No randomized study of organic founder videos by production level exists: render variants and learn per account.

## Where higher production pays

- B2B narrative: in LinkedIn's analysis of 13,000+ B2B video ads, cinematic narrative brand videos earned +129% engagement while short vertical "real talk" videos earned +103% dwell time ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/linkedin-study-b2b-video-ads-engagement-lift-458873)). Both work; they do different jobs.
- Explainers where the picture carries information (maps, data, product screens).
- When creative quality is above average: repurposed TV-style ads helped only then (MAGNA).
- The risk at level 4: effects that make a real person look synthetic (heavy keying, skin smoothing, gaze correction). Keep the speaker real.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Default | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | picture change + sound + text within 1 to 2 s; proposition stated by 3 s | measured (Meta x Toluna), platform guidance (TikTok) |
| Text hook vs spoken hook | one promise, different words (high meaning overlap, low word overlap) | key terms beat verbatim duplication in a 57-study meta-analysis ([Adesope and Nesbit, 2012](https://rex.libraries.wsu.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Verbal-Redundancy-in-Multimedia-Learning-Environments/99900601157101842)); practitioners stress alignment |
| Loudness | -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 to -1.5 dBTP | convention; no platform publishes a target |
| Music under voice | about 16 to 18 dB below the voice while speaking, about 10 dB in pauses | none found: tune per account, measure with the mix report |
| Sound effects | only on visual events | perception research: a tone synchronized with a visual change captures attention ([Van der Burg et al., 2008](https://research.vu.nl/ws/files/2321477/Burg%20Journal%20of%20Vision%208%285%29%202008%20u.pdf)) |
| Cut rhythm | cuts on speech boundaries and gestures; punch-ins on emphasis | cognitive film research: cuts are least visible when a cue precedes them ([Smith, 2012](https://ualresearchonline.arts.ac.uk/id/eprint/21187/2/6679.pdf)); no measured short-form rate |
| Safe areas (1080x1920) | text within y 269 to 1248 when possible; nothing in x 960 to 1080 below y 840; captions may sit lower when the face is low | vendor readings of Meta and TikTok specs ([Billo](https://billo.app/blog/meta-ads-safe-zones), [AdKit](https://adkit.so/tools/safe-zones/tiktok)) |
| Inserts | each one tied to the sentence it proves | measured for products (5.3x with context); a rule of judgment for the rest |

Practitioner extreme to avoid as a default: "retention editing" with a cut every 1.3 to 1.5 s, which its best-known practitioner publicly moved away from in 2024 ([Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240403-retention-editing-beastification-may-be-end)).

## Cost of each level (2 vCPUs, measured 2026-10-28 on a 90 s edit)

| Level | Agent time | Human equivalent (US agencies, vendor rates) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | about 3 min | $15 to $30 per basic edit |
| 2 | 5 to 10 min | $100 to $300 per clip |
| 3 | 15 to 25 min | $300 to $1,500 per performance ad |
| 4 | 40 to 50 min first pass, 20 min per new version | $120 to $180 per hour |

Rates: [beCreatives](https://becreatives.co/video-editing-prices/), [ShortVids](https://shortvids.co/video-editing-cost-breakdown-usa/), both agencies that sell editing.
