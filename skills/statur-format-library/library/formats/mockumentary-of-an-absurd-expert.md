# Mockumentary of an absurd expert

## Shape

A documentary-style portrait of a fictional expert who carries one absurd contradiction ("the only beekeeper allergic to bee stings"). The character speaks to an interviewer off camera, with workplace shots in between, all played seriously. The comedy comes from the straight face.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the character introduces themselves with the contradiction in one sentence.
2. 2 to 30 s: the daily routine, shot like a real documentary, the contradiction causing small problems.
3. 30 to 70 s: the escalation: the moment the contradiction becomes absurd, still played straight.
4. Last seconds: a serious closing statement from the character, and a final shot that contradicts it.

## Make it yours

Invent an expert whose contradiction is the problem your product solves. An AI calendar assistant films "the only executive assistant who has never once been on time". A cybersecurity startup portrays "the security expert whose password is on a sticky note". A translation app films "the international tour guide who speaks one language". Shoot it at your office or on location, and let the product appear once, in passing, as the thing the character refuses to use.

## Variations

- Series: one absurd expert per episode, same documentary style.
- Real team member: a colleague plays a heightened version of their own job.
- Interview cutaways: colleagues comment on the expert, mockumentary style.

## Why it works

The contradiction is a surprise that makes the viewer wonder how the character survives, and the straight delivery keeps the joke going. A strong, odd character is easy to describe to a friend, which helps sharing.

## Watch out

- Make it clear the expert is fictional, so nobody is mistaken for a real professional.
- High effort: write and rehearse. A weak script is more visible in this polished style.
- Avoid contradictions about health or disability; keep the absurdity in the job.
