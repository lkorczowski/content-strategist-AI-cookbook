# Celebrity clip, expert lens

## Shape

A clip of a famous person plays behind the expert on a greenscreen. The expert stops it at two or three moments and points out what only their trade can see: a word choice, a gesture, a decision. The viewer leaves seeing the famous moment differently.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the famous face on screen and a promise ("what you missed in this interview").
2. 2 to 30 s: first stop, first detail explained in plain words.
3. 30 to 70 s: the surprising detail, the one most viewers never noticed.
4. Last seconds: the verdict in one line and what the viewer can take for themselves.

## Make it yours

You have an expert lens, so point it at a famous founder. A pitch coaching app analyzes a well-known founder's keynote opening. An AI speech analytics tool shows the filler words and pauses in a famous product launch. A design tool for startups breaks down the slides of a celebrated investor presentation. Pick moments people already know, then add the one thing only your field notices.

## Variations

- Then and now: the same famous person years apart, analyzed with your lens.
- Viewer request: analyze the clip a commenter asks for.
- Two celebrities: the same skill compared on two famous people.

## Why it works

A familiar face stops the scroll, and the promise of a hidden detail opens an information gap (Loewenstein 1994). Repeating what the expert noticed lets viewers look smart in the next conversation (Berger 2013).

## Watch out

- Use short excerpts for commentary and check the platform's rules on clips of broadcasts and films.
- Analyze skills and choices. Comments on bodies, health or private life damage your brand.
- Avoid wording that suggests the celebrity endorses you.

```
Script a "celebrity clip, expert lens" video for [company], whose expertise is [field], for [audience].
1. Suggest 5 well-known public clips (keynotes, interviews, pitches) where [field] reveals something most viewers miss.
2. For the best one, write: the opening promise (under 10 words), 2 or 3 timestamps to pause on with the detail explained at each, the most surprising detail, and the closing verdict plus one takeaway for [audience].
3. Add greenscreen layout notes.
Rules: short excerpts used for commentary, analyze skills and choices only, no implied endorsement by the celebrity.
```
