# Founder responds to a review

## Shape

The founder plays a creator's review of their own product, pausing after each criticism. They answer point by point: they agree where the reviewer is right, explain the trade-off behind each choice, and say what is coming next. The video ends on the one point the founder is proudest of.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the reviewer's sharpest line plays first, the founder visible in the corner or beside it.
2. 2 to 25 s: the founder pauses the clip and answers the first criticism. Start with one you agree with.
3. 25 to 50 s: the trade-off behind a design choice, said in plain words, with what you are doing about it.
4. Last 5 to 10 s: the reviewer's best compliment, a short thank you, and what ships next.

## Make it yours

Search your product name on TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn and pick a review with real criticism. You can answer it as the founder: an AI note-taker for doctors answers "it misses accents", a project tool for agencies answers "too expensive for a team of three", an AI resume builder answers "the templates all look the same". Concede first, then explain. The admission is what makes the defense credible.

## Variations

- Brand account version: the company reacts to a well-known reviewer's awards or ranking video (see the second example).
- Bad review on a store page: show a one-star review as greenscreen and answer it the same way.
- Series: one review per week, same opening, so viewers come back for the next answer.

## Why it works

Watching a founder face criticism live creates tension: will they get defensive? Agreeing in public breaks the expected script, and sharing it lets the viewer look in the know about the product (Berger 2013, social currency).

## Watch out

- Use the platform's duet or stitch tools, or get the reviewer's permission before reusing their clip.
- Never attack or mock the reviewer. A defensive reply turns into a second wave of criticism.
- Disclose any paid partnership if the review was sponsored.

## For your AI

```
Script a "founder responds to a review" video for [company], which makes [product] for [audience].
Input: the review transcript or the list of criticisms: [paste here].
1. Sort the criticisms into: fair (we agree), trade-off (we chose this on purpose), wrong (factually inaccurate).
2. Pick 2 or 3 points, starting with a fair one. Write the founder's answer to each in 2 sentences, plain words.
3. Give the opening clip to play (the sharpest line), the pause points, and the closing line (the best compliment, a thank you, what ships next).
4. Add shot notes: greenscreen or stitch layout, where the founder sits in the frame.
Rules: agree where the reviewer is right, never mock the reviewer, no promises about dates you cannot keep, credit the reviewer on screen.
```
