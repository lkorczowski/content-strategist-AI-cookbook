# Famous company story in 60 seconds

## Shape

The speaker tells the surprising arc of a well-known company as a story: the failure, the pivot, the outcome. Photos, headlines and footage play behind or between the lines. The first sentence compresses the whole arc into one twist, and the last sentence lands a punchline or a lesson.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the whole arc in one line, with the twist ("builds a game nobody plays, still sells the company for billions").
2. 2 to 40 s: the setup. Who, when, what they tried, and why it failed.
3. 40 to 120 s: the pivot, the one decision that changed everything, with one checkable detail.
4. Last seconds: the outcome and the punchline or the lesson for the viewer.

## Make it yours

You tell the stories of your own field, and each one carries a lesson your buyers care about. A founder of a B2B payments tool tells how a famous payments company started with a tiny feature. An AI analytics founder tells how a well-known product found its real use after a failed first version. A remote work software founder tells the story of a company that went fully remote by accident. Choose stories with a turn your audience can apply, and say the lesson in one line at the end.

## Variations

- Failure version: a famous company that collapsed, and the warning sign in hindsight.
- Your own pivot: the same arc told about your company, which turns it into a founder story.
- Series: one company per week from your industry, same opening rhythm.

## Why it works

A story with a setup, a turn and a payoff holds attention better than a list of facts, and the twist in the first line opens a gap the viewer wants to close (Loewenstein 1994). Stories carry ideas that viewers retell to others (Berger 2013, stories).

## Watch out

- Every fact must be checkable. Name your source when a number or a quote is central.
- Use photos and logos within fair use and platform rules. Avoid suggesting the company endorses you.
- Avoid stating guesses about private decisions as facts; say "reportedly" when it is reported.
