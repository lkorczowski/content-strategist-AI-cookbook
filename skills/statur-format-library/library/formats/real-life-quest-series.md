# Real-life quest series

## Shape

The creator sets a real goal with an uncertain outcome: find a stolen object, meet someone, reach a hard-to-reach person or place. Each episode follows one step of the search, with leads, setbacks and progress. Every episode ends with an open question, and viewers return for the ending.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the goal in one sentence, said to camera ("My motorbike was stolen, I'm trying to find it").
2. 2 to 30 s: a reminder of where the quest stands (the episode number, the last lead).
3. Body: this episode's step: a visit, a call, a clue, a dead end. Film it as it happens.
4. Last 5 to 15 s: a new lead or a cliffhanger, and a request for help from viewers if the quest needs it.

## Make it yours

Pick a real goal tied to your company and let the audience follow it. A founder of a recruiting platform looks for one rare profile and documents every step of the search. A founder of an AI travel planner tries to reach a remote place using only the product's plans. A founder of a B2B startup tries to get a meeting with one specific, well-known buyer and films each attempt. The outcome must stay open: the series works because nobody knows if you will make it.

## Variations

- Party quest: build an event around the goal, like the 70-year-old throwing a party to meet someone.
- Exploration quest: explore a strange place or system in parts, with a reveal promised at the end.
- Crowd quest: viewers send leads in the comments and each episode follows one.

## Why it works

An unfinished quest stays in the viewer's mind and pulls them back for the next part (Zeigarnik 1927). A real person chasing a real goal is a story, and stories with emotion are the ones people pass on (Berger 2013).

## Watch out

- The quest must be real. A staged ending breaks the trust the series built.
- Blur faces, plates and addresses of third parties, and avoid accusing anyone without proof.
- Plan the end: post a final episode even if the quest fails.

## For your AI

```
Plan a "real-life quest series" for [founder name], founder of [company], whose audience is [audience].
1. Propose 5 real goals with an uncertain outcome linked to [company] or [market], doable in 3 to 10 episodes. For each, say why the outcome is uncertain and how viewers can help.
2. For the best one, write the episode 1 opening line (under 15 words), the plan for episodes 1 to 5 (one step each), and the cliffhanger line of each episode.
3. Write the final episode plan for both outcomes: success and failure.
Rules: the quest is real, no staged ending, third parties blurred unless they consent, no accusation without proof.
```
