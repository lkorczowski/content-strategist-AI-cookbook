# Founder story in one moment

## Shape

The founder opens inside one scene from their past: a place, a date, a feeling. They tell that single moment like a short story, then link it in one or two sentences to why the company exists today. The name, the title and the résumé come last, or stay out.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the first line drops the viewer into the scene ("I was standing in a bank queue with a box of receipts").
2. 2 to 25 s: the moment, told with one or two concrete details and what the founder felt.
3. 25 to 45 s: the turn. What the founder decided that day, or what they understood.
4. Last 10 s: the link to the company today, in one sentence, and a question to the viewer about their own version of that moment.

## Make it yours

Pick the one moment you tell at every dinner when someone asks why you started. Tell one moment, and leave the biography for your About page. An AI bookkeeping tool for freelancers starts on the night the founder lost a client invoice. A scheduling app for clinics starts on the morning a nurse called eleven patients by hand. A code review assistant starts on the outage the founder caused in their first job. The product appears only as the answer to that moment.

## Variations

- Yapping in the car: same story, told raw to the front camera, one take.
- Told as a funny ad: the founder laughs at their past self, and the moment becomes the punchline that leads to the product (see the first example).
- Cinematic voice-over: the founder's voice over B-roll of the place where it happened.

## Why it works

A story with a scene and a feeling travels further than a list of facts, which matches the stories and emotion drivers in Berger 2013. Starting inside the moment raises a question (what happened next?) that keeps people watching.

## Watch out

- Opening on "Hi, I'm the founder of" loses the viewer before the story starts.
- Keep it true. Viewers and journalists check origin stories.
- Ask permission before naming anyone who appears in the moment.

## For your AI

```
Write a 30 to 60 second "founder story in one moment" script for [company], which makes [product] for [audience].
Here is my raw material: [the moment, the place, the date, what I felt, what I decided].
1. Write 3 opening lines that start inside the scene. No name, no title, no company in the first sentence.
2. Write the script in 4 beats: the scene, the detail and the feeling, the decision, the link to [company] today in one sentence.
3. Add shot notes: front camera or car, one take or light cuts, where to show one photo from that time if I have one.
4. End with one question that invites viewers to share their own moment in the comments.
Rules: keep every fact true, keep the product to one sentence, plain spoken words, under 150 words of script.
```
