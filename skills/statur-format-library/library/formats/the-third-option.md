# The third option

## Shape

The speaker describes the choice the viewer thinks they face: option A or option B, each with its pain. Then they reveal a third option most people have never heard of, explain how it works, and say who it fits. The video ends on the one condition that makes the third option the right pick.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the false choice in one line ("You think you have to choose between A and B").
2. 2 to 20 s: the cost of each side, quickly, so the viewer feels stuck.
3. 20 to 70 s: the third option. What it is, how it works, one concrete case.
4. Last seconds: who it is for, and who should still pick A or B.

## Make it yours

This is a positioning format for a new category. Your product is often the third option between two old ways of doing things. You can frame it like this: an AI sales assistant between "hire an SDR" and "do outreach yourself", a fractional finance platform between "a full-time CFO" and "a spreadsheet you dread", a no-code internal tool builder between "wait six months for engineering" and "buy rigid software". Explain the category first. Your product can appear at the end, as one way to get it.

## Variations

- Industry version: the third option is a method or a process, with no product at all, which builds trust before any pitch.
- Fourth option tease: end with "and there is a fourth one, for next time", to open a series.
- Quiz opener: ask viewers to pick A or B in the comments, then reveal C.

## Why it works

The opening line sets a gap: the viewer is told there is something they do not know about their own decision (Loewenstein 1994). A practical answer to a real dilemma is worth saving and sending to a colleague facing the same choice (Berger 2013, practical value).

## Watch out

- The false choice must be one your audience actually feels. An invented dilemma sounds like an ad.
- Say honestly who the third option does not fit. That sentence builds more trust than the pitch.
- Keep regulated topics (money, health, legal) general, and point to a professional for personal advice.
