# Bad buzz answered one viral video at a time

## Shape

When videos attacking the company or its product go viral, the founder answers them one by one, as reactions. Each video plays a few seconds of one attack, then the founder says what is fair in it and answers one argument with a fact. The series starts with the most watched attack and moves down the list.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: a few seconds of the viral attack, with a textbox that names it plainly ("Yes, this video is about us").
2. 2 to 20 s: the founder says what is true or fair in the criticism.
3. 20 to 60 s: one argument answered with one fact, a number with its source, or a demo.
4. Last seconds: what the company changes or what the viewer should know, and the next video in the series.

## Make it yours

Silence leaves the story to others, so you use the attention while it is there. An AI note-taker accused of leaking data answers the top video with exactly what is stored and where. A scheduling app mocked in a viral rant agrees on the bug and shows the fix. An e-scooter startup caught in a viral safety rant runs the brake test on camera and shares the results. Publish at least one answer the same day, then longer technical videos once you are seen as the voice on the other side. This shape was shot in a real program, during a real bad buzz.

## Variations

- Long form after: a full explanation on YouTube or a live, once the short answers have reach.
- Press angle: the first answer that travels often brings interview requests; prepare one paragraph for journalists.
- Team voice: an engineer answers the technical attacks, the founder answers the rest.

## Why it works

People share the backlash and then share the answer, because seeing both sides makes them look informed (Berger 2013). Agreeing on the fair part first lowers defenses, so the fact that follows is heard.

## Watch out

- Never attack the creators. Answer the argument, with a calm tone and no corporate speak.
- Do not show details that invite escalation (how to break or bypass something).
- Coach the founder before filming and check legal exposure on claims about people or contracts.
