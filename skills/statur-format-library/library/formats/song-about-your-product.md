# Song about your product

## Shape

An original song or a parody tells the product's job or the customer's daily pain, over b-roll of the product in use. Verses describe the problem in a funny, specific way. The chorus is the product promise, short enough to stick.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the first line of the chorus or the most catchy line, sung over the clearest product shot.
2. 2 to 20 s: verse one, the pain, with b-roll that matches each line.
3. 20 to 45 s: the chorus, the promise, the product in action.
4. Last seconds: the chorus line one more time, ending on the product.

## Make it yours

Write a short song about the pain your user feels and the relief your product brings. A shared calendar app writes a chorus about never double-booking again. An AI code reviewer writes a ballad about the pull request nobody wants to read. A pet tracking device writes a song about knowing where your cat is. You can sing yourself, or work with a musician and keep b-roll of the product on screen.

## Variations

- Parody: new lyrics over an original melody you composed in the style of a known genre.
- Long version: a full parody song for YouTube, cut into shorts (see the second example).
- Customer chorus: customers sing one line each, with consent.

## Why it works

A melody is easy to remember and repeat, and a funny, specific lyric gives people something to quote and share (Berger 2013). The chorus repeats the promise without feeling like an ad.

## Watch out

- Write your own lyrics and music. Protected lyrics and melodies need a license, even for a parody on a brand account.
- Keep the promise in the chorus accurate and free of results you cannot show.
- Check commercial use rights for any backing track.

## For your AI

```
Write a "song about your product" for [company], whose product [product] solves [problem] for [audience].
1. List 5 specific, funny details of [problem] in the life of [audience].
2. Write original lyrics: a chorus of 2 to 4 lines that states what [product] does, one verse of 4 to 8 lines about the pain, and b-roll notes for each line. Suggest a genre and tempo.
Rules: original lyrics and melody only, no protected lyrics, accurate promise with no promised results, commercial-cleared backing track.
```
