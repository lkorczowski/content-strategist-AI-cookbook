# Deadpan sigma coach parody

## Shape

A team member speaks to camera like a self-proclaimed "sigma" coach: slow voice, total seriousness, grand claims about discipline and status. The subject is an ordinary product, treated as the mark of a superior person ("You are a sigma. Your spreadsheet does not show it."). Objections are mocked, the call to action lands, then a long pause and a punchline.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the coach stares at the camera and delivers the first grand line about the viewer.
2. 2 to 20 s: the product presented as a life philosophy, with slow b-roll of it in use.
3. 20 to 35 s: two objections mocked in the same flat voice, then the call to action.
4. Last seconds: a silent pause, then the punchline that breaks the tone once.

## Make it yours

The more ordinary the product, the stronger the contrast. A project management tool becomes "the board of a man who finishes what he starts". An AI email assistant becomes "inbox zero is a state of being". A password manager becomes "a sigma never resets his password". The speaker can be anyone on the team who holds a straight face. A colleague working in the background without reacting adds a second layer of comedy.

## Variations

- Background boss: the founder keeps working behind the speaker and never reacts.
- Pre-roll cut: a 60-second version for YouTube ads, same pause and punchline.
- Duo: two coaches compete over who owns the more sigma version of the product.

## Why it works

The format parodies a genre most viewers have seen in their feed, so the joke is recognized in the first line. Deadpan delivery makes people wait for the moment the speaker breaks, which keeps them to the end.

## Watch out

- Speak slowly and never smile until the punchline; one tone break only.
- Mock the genre, never a real person or a group of people.
- Check that the parody of a show or a sound does not reuse protected footage or music without rights.
