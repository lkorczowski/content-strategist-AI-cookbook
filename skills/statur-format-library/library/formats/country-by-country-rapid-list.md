# Country by country rapid list

## Shape

The speaker goes through a series of groups (countries, personas, buyer types) and gives one rule for each in one sentence. The pattern repeats: "If you're selling to X, do this. To Y, never do that." There is no intro and no wrap-up, only the list delivered fast.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the first rule, straight away, with the group named on screen.
2. 2 to 40 s: four to six more groups, one sentence each, same sentence pattern, a cut between each.
3. 40 to 65 s: the group your audience belongs to, or the one that surprises most, held for the strongest rule.
4. Last seconds: ask viewers which group you forgot, or end on the most counterintuitive rule.

## Make it yours

Swap countries for the buyer types you meet every week. A sales intelligence platform runs "If you're pitching a CFO, a CTO, a head of sales". A developer tool runs one rule per programming community. A recruiting AI for startups runs one rule per candidate type. Each rule must come from your own calls and deals, and you can say so on screen. Viewers share the part that names their own group.

## Variations

- Persona edition: one rule per job title instead of per country.
- Mistake edition: one mistake per group, delivered as "never".
- Series: one video per region or per industry, same opening line.

## Why it works

Each line is a piece of practical value a viewer can repeat at work, and sharing a rule about one's own group makes the sharer look informed, two drivers Berger 2013 lists. The repeated sentence pattern makes the next line easy to anticipate, so people stay for their group.

## Watch out

- Stereotypes presented as facts break the format and hurt people. Frame each rule as your experience ("in my deals with...") and keep it about business habits.
- One wrong rule about a culture gets corrected loudly in the comments. Check each one with someone from that group.
- Keep it about buyers and processes, never about ethnicity or personal traits.

## For your AI

```
Write a "country by country rapid list" video for [company], which sells [product] to [audience].
1. Pick the groups: [countries, job titles or buyer types] that [audience] deals with.
2. For each group, write one rule in one sentence, using the pattern "If you're selling to X, do this" or "To Y, never do that". Base each rule on [my experience or data: describe it].
3. Order them so the most surprising rule comes second to last. Give the opening line (the first rule, no intro) and shot notes (facecam, cut between groups, group name on screen).
4. Flag any rule that could read as a stereotype and rewrite it as a business habit from experience.
Rules: no stereotypes about people, only buying habits; every rule must come from real experience; 45 to 80 seconds.
```
