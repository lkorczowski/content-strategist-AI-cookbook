# Contrarian hot take with proof

## Shape

The speaker opens by telling the viewer they are doing something wrong, or that they should skip what everyone does. The next line is personal proof: what the speaker did or tried. Then the reasoning in three short beats, to camera.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the contrarian line, addressed to "you" ("You're probably running your demos wrong").
2. 2 to 10 s: the proof. One personal fact that earns the right to the opinion ("I was too, until...", "After 200 sessions...").
3. 10 to 50 s: the reasoning in three beats: what people believe, what actually happens, what to do instead.
4. Last seconds: a one-line verdict that repeats the take, so the video loops back to the opening.

## Make it yours

Find the habit in your market that your product exists to change, and attack the habit with respect for the people who hold it. An AI meeting notes tool says founders should stop taking notes in calls, after the founder reviewed their own call recordings. A no-code analytics product says early startups should skip hiring a data analyst, after the founder ran the numbers themselves for two years. An AI recruiting tool says cover letters waste everyone's time, backed by the founder's years of hiring. The take must be one you would defend in a meeting with a skeptical customer.

## Variations

- "I was wrong too": the speaker confesses they made the same mistake, which softens the attack (see the second example).
- Career take: the opinion targets a life choice of the audience (see the first example), stronger and riskier.
- Stitch version: the take answers a popular video that says the opposite.

## Why it works

A claim that contradicts what the viewer believes opens a gap between what they know and what they just heard, the curiosity mechanism described by Loewenstein 1994. The personal proof makes it hard to dismiss, and disagreement in the comments feeds distribution.

## Watch out

- A hot take needs a real argument. Rage bait without substance burns trust and invites pile-ons.
- Attack a practice and leave individuals out of it.
- Your proof must be true and checkable.
