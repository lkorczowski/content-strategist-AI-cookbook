# POV skit: the product saves the moment

## Shape

"POV:" text on screen sets an everyday crisis. The scene is filmed in first person, so the viewer lives the problem. Then the product arrives and saves the moment, in one quick, visible action.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: "POV:" caption with the crisis ("POV: the client call starts in 30 seconds and your slides are gone").
2. 2 to 10 s: first-person panic, small details that make it real.
3. 10 to 22 s: the product appears and fixes it in one action.
4. Last seconds: the relief shot, the moment saved.

## Make it yours

Pick a small crisis your user knows by heart and film it from their eyes. An AI slide generator saves the five-minutes-before-the-pitch moment. A password manager saves the "locked out before a deadline" moment. A translation earbud startup saves a lost traveller asking for directions. Keep it short: the crisis, the save, the relief.

## Variations

- Fail first: the first attempt without the product fails, then the product saves it.
- Customer POV: a real customer films their own save, with consent.
- Series: a new crisis each week, the same "POV:" caption style.

## Why it works

The first-person view puts the viewer in the problem, so the save feels like their own relief. The crisis is familiar, which makes viewers recognize themselves and share it.

## Watch out

- The save must be what the product really does, at real speed.
- When judging results, look at saves and shares. High views with few likes can mean paid reach.
- Brand accounts disclose paid placements and use commercial-cleared sounds.
