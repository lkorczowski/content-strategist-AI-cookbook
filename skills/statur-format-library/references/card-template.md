# Distinctive element + structure (twist or ending)

## Shape

One or two sentences: what happens on screen, in order. Specific enough that two people would shoot the same thing.

## Beats

1. 0 to 2 s: the opening, word for word or as an on-screen action.
2. 2 to 20 s: the body.
3. 20 to 50 s: the turn or the proof.
4. Last seconds: the ending (payoff, call to comment, demo, loop back to the start).

## Make it yours

How a SaaS or AI founder turns this shape into a video about their market, their product or their story. One concrete example with an invented company.

## Variations

- A variation changes one element (setup, length, ending) and keeps the shape.

## Why it works

One to three sentences. A primary source when a fact is claimed.

## Watch out

- Up to three things that break the format or break a platform rule.
