# Format Library taxonomy

Generated by `scripts/library.py build` from `taxonomy.json`. Edit the JSON, then run the script.

Version 0.1, 2026-10-25.

Every card carries tags on four dimensions. A card can carry several tags per dimension, and the dimensions combine freely: a founder story can be shot as yapping in a car or as a cinematic voice-over, and work at the top or the middle of the funnel.

Why four dimensions: one flat list of tags mixes things that answer different questions (where it works, why people share it, what happens, how it is shot). Separated, they let anyone filter by goal (BOFU), by constraint (no face, low effort) or by skill (screen recording only), and they make names systematic.

## Naming rule

Name = distinctive element + structure + (twist or ending). In English, short enough to search.

- "Smart-glasses street prank (demo at the end)": the glasses are the distinctive element, the prank is the structure, the demo is the ending.
- Same structure and setup, another topic: same card, new example.
- Same structure, but a setup or an ending that changes the beats: new card.

## Other fields

- **origin**: mimetic = Copies a shape that already performs on the platform.; original = Built around one person, place or product, hard to copy.
- **founder_on_camera**: yes = The founder must be on camera.; no = Works without the founder's face.; optional = Works both ways.
- **effort**: low = Phone, under 30 minutes from idea to export.; medium = Half a day: script, shoot, edit.; high = Crew, actors, locations or heavy edit.
- **status**: draft = Described from public examples.; tested = Shot at least once in a real program.; proven = Kept after a format review on mature data.
