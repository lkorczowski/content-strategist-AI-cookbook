# <case folder> · annotation brief

Paths are relative to the folder that holds `<case folder>/`. Each video has a folder `<case folder>/videos/NN-<id>/` with:
- `meta.json` (url, date, duration_s, views, likes, comments, shares, saves, caption) and `work/units-asr.tsv` (the units: id, start, end, track, words, text), both generated by `<skill>/scripts/short_units.py` from the transcript;
- `02-map.md`, a skeleton with every unit already listed (id, time, incipit) and every tag column set to "?".

The transcript with cue times is in `<case folder>/source/transcripts/NN-<id>.txt`. The groups follow `<case folder>/source/selection.md`: the most-viewed videos are group "top", the most recent ones are group "recent".

Your videos: `<assigned videos>`. Your scratch folder: `<scratch folder>` (helper files go there and nowhere else).

## Read first
1. `<skill>/references/taxonomy.md` (all codes) and `<skill>/references/taxonomy-short.md` (short beats, hook and ending codes).
2. The gold example, tagged by the lead analyst: `<case folder>/videos/<gold video>/` (01-blocks.md, 02-map.md, 03-loops.md, card.json). Match its granularity and style: one primary function, up to three secondary ones, figures only when present, loops as `L01o` (opened) and `L01c` (closed), `-` for empty.

## Per video, write
1. `01-blocks.md`: first line after the title `hook: H-... · end: E-... · turn at mm:ss (NN%) · template: <beat string>`, then the machine table `| blk | start | end | beat | threads | title | job |` with one row per beat (B00, B01...), times from the units, beats from taxonomy-short.md (HOOK, FRAME, SETUP, TURN, BUILD, PAYOFF, BUTTON, STORY, ASIDE, CTA). A video can have several TURN or BUILD blocks. Then 2 to 4 lines of reading.
2. `02-map.md`: replace every "?" (blk = the B## of the beat that contains the unit; trk stays NAR; thr S/L/H/A/X with S = a story or historical/real episode told as events, L = the argument or lesson, H = the creator as a person, A = the viewer addressed or simulated, X = channel or meta). Never change ids, times or the incipit. If a unit is an automatic-caption fragment that belongs to the next sentence, tag it with the same function as the next unit.
3. `03-loops.md`: table `| id | open_t | open_u | close_t | close_u | type | scale | description |`, scales MASTER (most of the video), ARC (a beat or more), MICRO (closes within about 10 s), OPEN (never closed). Short videos usually have 3 to 8 loops.
4. `card.json`: `{"group": "top" or "recent", "topic": "...", "hook": "H-...", "end": "E-...", "turn_t": "mm:ss", "one_line": "...", "signature_moves": ["...", ...], "scores": {"CUR":..,"WEA":..,"CLA":..,"HUM":..,"EMO":..,"AUT":..,"PAY":..,"VOI":..}}`. signature_moves = 4 to 8 short, reusable descriptions of what this video does (for example "voices the viewer's objection in quotes, then answers").
5. Run `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/analyze.py <case folder>/videos/<folder> <duration_s from meta.json>` and fix your tables until it runs without error.

## Rules
- Tag what the words do. When the creator states a view in order to knock it down, the TURN is where the knock-down happens. Tag the stated view OBJECTION, or CONCEDE with IRONY when it is voiced ironically.
- Leave transcripts, meta.json and units-asr.tsv as they are. Run no git command. Write only in your assigned video folders and your scratch folder.
- Describe sensitive topics (crime, politics, health) neutrally in glosses.
- Transcripts stay private: quote at most a short phrase in a gloss.
