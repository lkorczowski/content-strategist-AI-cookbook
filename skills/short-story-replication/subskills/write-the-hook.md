# Write the hook

Start from your author's hook counts in `10-author-grammar.md`, overall and inside the chosen template. Draft the two or three types the author uses most. Whatever the type, open a loop in the first 5 seconds.

## Types, with the move behind each (examples are invented)
- H-CLAIM with a verdict word: a superlative or a judgment about something on screen. "This is the most expensive spreadsheet in the company." The word is the promise.
- H-QUESTION with the verdict inside: "Why does every onboarding email sound like a ransom note?" The question and the judgment arrive together.
- H-NEWS: a post or an article on screen, read in one line, then the reaction.
- H-CONTRA: calls a celebrated habit a trap, a famous thing overrated. "Inbox zero is a trap."
- H-CONFESS: admits something about yourself the viewer did not expect. "I deleted my best-performing video last week."

## Tests
1. Can a viewer repeat it after one hearing? Under 15 words wins.
2. Does it contain a word the rest of the video must earn? Circle it; the button should come back to it.
3. Is the image on screen the same thing the sentence talks about? Put the object of the hook on screen in the first second.
4. Would it still be true if the video stopped after the turn? A hook that needs the button to be true is a bait.
