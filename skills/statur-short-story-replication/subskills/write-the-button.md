# Write the button

Start from your author's ending counts in `10-author-grammar.md`, overall and inside the chosen template. Draft the button in the template's most common ending type first.

## Moves (examples are invented)
- Push the hook word to its extreme: "the most expensive spreadsheet in the company" becomes "the only spreadsheet with its own budget line".
- Callback to the object: "Nine years later, the temporary fix is still running."
- The sincere drop: after a joking video, one plain sentence of what the creator believes.
- Hand the question to the comments only when the mystery is real, for example a puzzle the viewer can try.

## Tests
1. Does it reuse the hook's word or image?
2. Is it shorter than the sentence before it?
3. Does it add something? A button that summarizes closes nothing.
4. Read it in the target voice: if the author's form breaks a voice rule, use voice-adaptation.md.
