# Hook forensics

Part of The X Ghostwriter Handbook (https://statur.co/x-ghostwriter/), recipe 03. For short videos, use statur-author-analysis and statur-story-analysis instead: they read a script beat by beat. This skill reads text posts and X Articles.

The job: understand how a post earned its reach, in words a writer can reuse, without copying anyone's text.

## Single responsibility

This skill studies posts and maintains the formula file. It never drafts the founder's posts (statur-x-viral-post-ghostwriter), never collects the founder's own metrics (statur-social-analytics) and never maps the ideas of a controversy (statur-idea-war-map).

## 0. Read the context first

Read the company context file (refusal list, pillars, one reader) and the existing formula file. A formula that needs a move the founder refuses is still recorded, with "skip" for this brand.

## 1. Pick the cases

- 10 to 20 posts from accounts the one reader follows, on the founder's pillars, from the last 30 days. Include a few posts that underperformed on the same accounts: they show what the formula does not explain.
- The founder may send posts. Read each one fully, with its image, video or quoted post.
- Read public pages only, at a human pace. Never like, follow or reply while reading.

## 2. Read each case

For each post, write a case entry:
1. URL, author, date, type (post, reply, quote, thread, X Article), media.
2. Public counts at reading time and the hours since posting: views, replies, reposts, likes, bookmarks. Bookmarks above likes signal a reference people save. Flag promoted posts (website card, "Ad" label) and paid partnerships: their views say nothing about organic reach.
3. The first line in your own words, and what it does to the reader (self-implication, number, curiosity gap, identity, conflict, promise of a reference).
4. The techniques used further down: structure, proof, objection handling, call to action, where the link sits.
5. Which ranked actions the post invites, per references/x-ranker.md of statur-x-viral-post-ghostwriter: replies, quotes, follows, link copies.
6. Claims to check before anyone reuses them: unverifiable results, vendor numbers, windows chosen after the fact.
7. Reuse and skip, for this brand.

## 3. Update the formula file

- A formula gets a code (F1, F2...), a one-line pattern with placeholders, what it does to the reader, the cases that use it, and a verdict for this brand (use, test, skip, with the reason).
- A formula enters the table when two or more cases use it. A single case stays in the case list.
- Every quarter, remove formulas no recent case uses and mark the ones the founder's own posts tested, with the result.

## Output

The formula file (templates/hook-formulas.md), then a short note: the 3 formulas to test this week and the case behind each.

## Rules

- Drafts only. Never post, like, follow or reply while studying posts.
- Never ask for a password. Public pages only, human pace, no bulk collection: respect X's terms.
- Copy no one's text. Describe a technique in your own words, quote at most a few words to name it, and link the post.
- No invented numbers: every count has its reading date and the hours since posting. A post's claimed result is a claim until checked.
- Attack ideas, never people.
