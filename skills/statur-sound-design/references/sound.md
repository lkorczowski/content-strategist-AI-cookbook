# Sound sources and licenses (checked 2026-10-28)

General information, not legal advice. Check the terms page before each new use.

| Source | Use in a commercial social video | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| sfx_synth.py, music_bed.py | yes, generated here | none |
| Freesound | per sound: CC0 yes, CC BY with credit, CC BY-NC no | read each sound's license ([FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq)) |
| Pixabay music and effects | allowed for commercial video | some tracks are registered with Content ID: keep the download page and license with the asset ([FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)) |
| Epidemic Sound, Artlist | with a subscription | Artlist forbids automated downloading by bots ([license](https://help-center.artlist.io/privacy-terms/artlist-license)) |
| TikTok Commercial Music Library | inside TikTok, at posting time | not in the master file ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library?lang=en)) |
| YouTube Audio Library | inside YouTube | terms outside YouTube are narrower ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882?hl=en)) |
| ElevenLabs sound effects and music | paid plans grant commercial rights | needs the user's key ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform/llms.txt)) |
| Suno | paid plans, capped downloads since 2026-09-03 | free plan has no commercial rights ([Suno](https://suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos)) |
| MusicGen, AudioGen, MMAudio weights | no: non-commercial (CC BY-NC) | |
| ACE-Step 1.5 | repository MIT; weight terms to verify | runs on a Mac or a GPU |

Voice tools: RNNoise models for ffmpeg arnndn ([models](https://github.com/GregorR/rnnoise-models)); DeepFilterNet3 (MIT or Apache-2.0) is stronger and slower (about 2.4x realtime on 2 vCPUs).
