# Scoring rubric for short videos (v1)

Same 8 abilities as the long-form rubric (references/scoring.md of the video-story-analysis skill) so Fiches stay comparable; anchors are per minute and per video. Modifier = floor((score - 10) / 2).

## Anchors (out of 20; 10 = a competent creator, 18 = the best short you have seen)

| Ability | 6 | 10 | 14 | 18 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Curiosity (CUR) | no open question in the first 10 s | a hook loop closed in the first third, then nothing pending | a master loop from the first 10 s to the turn, about 2 loops per minute | a master loop from second 3 to the last line, 3+ loops per minute, never nothing pending |
| Weave (WEA) | one thread | two threads in long blocks | story or example, lesson and viewer alternate | 4 threads interleaved, a switch every 10 s or so |
| Clarity (CLA) | hard to follow | clear but meandering | one idea per sentence, plain words | every term defined at once, steps shown, zero jargon |
| Humor (HUM) | none | about 1 per minute | about 2 per minute | 4+ per minute, several techniques |
| Emotion (EMO) | flat | mean tension about 2 | a 4 in the last third | mean 3+, a 5 at the end |
| Authority (AUT) | no facts | a number or two | dated facts and named sources | sourced numbers throughout, objections anticipated, admits what is unknown |
| Payoff (PAY) | ends without closing | closes the hook loop | a button line that reframes | a button that closes the master loop with a callback or twist and changes the meaning of the video |
| Voice (VOI) | neutral | opinions at start and end | first-person stance, a confession | unmistakable voice: signature phrasing, irony, a stance every few sentences |

## Calibration

Calibrate on your own sample.
1. The lead analyst scores the gold video first and writes its 8 scores in the annotation brief. Every annotator reads them before scoring.
2. After the aggregation, read the ability table in `10-author-grammar.md` (mean, min and max per ability across the videos).
3. The blind re-tag of step 8 measures agreement: count the scores that differ by 2 points or more, and the mean gap.
4. When an annotator applies an anchor differently, write the clarified reading in the brief and rescore the videos it touches.
