# Video Script Structure

Script founder videos that people finish, from 2 minutes to 40: map the structure of any video you admire, then replay it on your own subject with sourced facts.

- Page (human version, source of truth): https://statur.co/video-script-structure/ (publication pending)
- Markdown for AI assistants: https://statur.co/video-script-structure.md (once the page is live)
- Version 1.1, 2026-10-26

## Summary

Retention is written before it is shot. Cut a video into sentences and tag each one on nine tiers: track, thread, function, figure, mechanism, objective, tension, open and closed questions, connective. The map shows what holds viewers: a question opened in the first seconds and answered by the last line, at least one question open at every moment, a turn placed early, and escalation with one sourced detail per beat. For short videos, map about twenty videos of one creator and name their templates. Then write your own script on the same beats, at the same pace and density, in your own voice, map it the same way and check parity before you film. Two quick reads sit on top of the map: the story ladder (reporter, magician, fortune teller, architect, maestro) gives the level a video reaches and the next rung to add, and the sentence rhythm check finds runs of long sentences that need a short one.
