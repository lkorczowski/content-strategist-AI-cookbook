# The X Ghostwriter Handbook

Grow on X from zero with an AI that drafts in your voice and a human who decides: brand DNA from evidence, hook forensics, the ranker's published weights, a reply routine for cold accounts, and a 30-day goal judged on business numbers.

## Summary

Write a refusal list of checkable "I never" lines and a voice mix in percentages from your own posts. Derive pillars from what a buyer must believe before paying. Study ten viral posts of your niche and keep a hook formula file. Read what X's open-source ranker counts: a copy of the post's link weighs 20, a reply 5 (20 on your original posts shown to mutual follows), a follow 4, a profile click 0. Draft with an AI, score every draft against your refusal list, and split the week into reach posts, revenue posts and replies. Under 1,000 followers, grow through replies and one X Article a week. Track the clicks the ranker ignores yourself, give the AI one 30-day goal and judge it on a scorecard.
