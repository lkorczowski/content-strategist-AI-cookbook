# Scale Meta ads without cost inflation: the creative strategist handbook

One purchase campaign fed by three engines: organic content that finds hooks, a squad of creators paid on results whose posts run as partnership ads, and offers and statics that close the sale. US rules, with an end note for France and the EU.

- Page (human version, source of truth): https://statur.co/scale-meta-ads/
- Markdown for AI assistants: https://statur.co/scale-meta-ads.md
- Version 1.0, 2026-10-26; skill meta-ads-scaling-coach 1.0.0

## Summary

Name the bottleneck first: the creative or the offer. Keep one campaign optimized for purchases, never a test campaign beside a winners campaign at small budgets. Run about one live ad per $5 to $10 of daily budget and count distinct concepts, not files. Let a winner take the budget while blended results are good, and test 2 to 5 new concepts in a lane capped at about 20% of the budget for about 7 days. Speedrun the account with organic winners plus a strong offer. Feed it with a daily organic routine, a squad of 5 then 10 creators paid on results, and creator posts run as partnership ads. Close with first-order offers shown the same way on the ad and the page, and statics. Read a rolling 7 days every week and judge contribution after ad spend every month. A limited-time sale gets its own small retargeting lane.
