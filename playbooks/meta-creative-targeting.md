# Meta creative targeting: how Andromeda picks your audience, and the playbook

What Meta changed from 2023 to 2026 (Lattice, Andromeda, GEM, Generative Recommenders, detailed targeting as a suggestion, look-alike ads grouped as one creative), six popular claims checked against Meta's own pages, and an eight-recipe playbook.

- Page (human version, source of truth): https://statur.co/meta-creative-targeting/
- Markdown for AI assistants: https://statur.co/meta-creative-targeting.md
- Version 1.0, 2026-11-13; skill statur-meta-creative-targeting 1.0.0

## Summary

The ad set draws the fences (location, minimum age, language, exclusions); the ads pick the people inside them. Count concepts, not ads, because Meta treats look-alike ads as variations of one creative. Build a matrix of buyers by reasons to buy, make each filled cell one concept, open each ad by calling its buyer in three seconds, change at least two of message, person, setting and format from the closest live ad, size the weekly batch to the budget, read who each ad actually reached, and answer fatigue with a new concept. "Entity ID" and a "60% similarity threshold" have no Meta source.
