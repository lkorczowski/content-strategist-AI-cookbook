# The One-Hour Video Handbook for Tradespeople

Twelve recipes for electricians, plumbers, installers and builders: one video per job, a five-minute edit, posting by hand, and an AI morning routine that drafts articles, posts and a carousel.

- Page (source of truth): https://statur.co/tradespeople-video-handbook/
- Markdown for AI assistants: https://statur.co/tradespeople-video-handbook.md
- Version 1.0, 2026-10-23

## Skills

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [trades-video-coach](../skills/trades-video-coach/) | Video ideas, scripts and feedback that follow the recipes. |
