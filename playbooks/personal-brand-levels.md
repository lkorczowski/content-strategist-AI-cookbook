# The 5 levels of personal branding: the Korczowski scale

A scale of personal branding for founders, from Type 0 (no time to post) to Type 4 (a generational public figure), measured by how far a person's ideas travel when they are not in the room. Inspired by the Kardashev scale of civilizations.

- Page (human version, source of truth): https://statur.co/personal-brand-levels/
- Markdown for AI assistants: https://statur.co/personal-brand-levels.md
- Version 1.0, 2026-10-29 (page); skill statur-personal-brand-scale 1.0.0

## Summary

Type 0, the invisible expert: ideas stay in the head and in meetings. Type 1, the grinder: systematic posting on one platform, stuck in the 300-view jail, the stage where content feels like a waste of time. Type 2, the niche authority: content brings DMs, deals and investor intros, one format is recognized, a team of one or two, the right time to hire a head of content. Type 3, the industry face: press and events outside the niche, a full-time team, ideas adopted by the industry. Type 4, the magnate: mainstream, generational, an audience that follows across industries. A 16-line self-test gives a decimal score; each type comes with its trap and the steps to the next one.
